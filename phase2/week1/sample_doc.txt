  The Reliability pillar encompasses the ability of a workload to perform its intended function correctly and
  consistently when it is expected to. This includes the ability to operate and test the workload through its total
  lifecycle. A resilient workload recovers quickly from failures to meet business and customer demand. Key topics include
  distributed system design, recovery planning, and adapting to changing requirements. Foundational requirements are
  those whose scope extends beyond a single workload or project. Before architecting any system, you must ensure that you
  have sufficient network bandwidth and other foundational requirements in place. Amazon Web Services provides service
  quotas, also referred to as limits, which are the maximum number of service resources or operations for your account.
  Change management is how you monitor and respond to changes in demand. Failure management includes backing up data,
  designing for fault isolation, and planning for disaster recovery.
